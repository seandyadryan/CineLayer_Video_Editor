1. Who we are

CineLayer Video Editor (the "app") is provided by PT Deploy Dulu Pulang Nanti ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains how the app accesses and uses information and how you can contact us about privacy.

The app does not require an account. It has no advertising, analytics, telemetry, or developer-operated media upload service. The app does not request Android's internet permission.

2. Your media and local library

When you import files, the app accesses only the videos, audio files, and images you select through Android's system file picker. It reads file contents to display previews, play media, and export a video trim when you request one.

The app reads file information such as the filename, media type, size, and available media details, including duration, frame rate, and aspect ratio. It stores local library entries, file references, favorites, notes and color tags you add, and saved trim information in private app preferences so your library can remain available between sessions. Searches across filenames, notes, color tags, frame rates, and aspect ratios, as well as source and media filters, are processed locally.

The app also includes bundled demonstration media marked Sample, including short clips, an ambient audio sample, and an AI-generated coastal image. These are supplied with the app. Using them does not upload information to an AI service or require an AI service connection.

Selected media may contain personal information, including voices, faces, or embedded metadata. The app does not send that information, your search text, or your library to us. We do not sell your information or use your media for advertising or AI training.

3. File access and permissions

You choose which files the app can use. Where supported, the app keeps Android's permission to reopen a selected file after a restart. This permission grants access to that file; it is not permission to scan your entire device.

When you export a trimmed video, Android asks you to choose where to save it. The app writes the MP4 to that chosen destination. It does not require access to your contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

4. Exports, document providers, and backups

Your original files remain in their existing locations. Exported videos are saved separately to the destination you select and may include source media content or metadata. Review an export before sharing it with someone else.

Android's picker may display local storage or a cloud storage provider installed on your device. If you choose a cloud-backed source or destination, that provider may download, upload, or synchronize the file under its own privacy policy. This is separate from the app's local processing; we do not receive a copy.

Your device, storage provider, or backup settings may also copy or synchronize original files and exports. Those services operate under their own settings and policies. Clearing the app's data does not remove copies stored elsewhere.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Library entries and preferences: library references, favorites, notes, color tags, and saved trim information are kept on your device until you remove them, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Android may offer device-transfer or restore behavior depending on your device and settings.
  • Original media: removing a library entry does not delete the original file. Use your file manager or storage provider to delete it.
  • Exported videos: remain in the location you chose until you delete them using your file manager or storage provider. They can remain after you clear app storage or uninstall.
  • File access: clearing app data or uninstalling removes the app's saved state and access grants. Copies held by backup or storage providers must be managed with those providers.

We do not hold a server-side copy of your media or local library and cannot remotely retrieve or delete files on your device.

6. Security

The app uses Android's application sandbox for its private preferences and Android's file-access controls for selected media. Access to originals and exports also depends on your device lock, storage permissions, and the provider you select. No device or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

The app does not transmit your media to us. Protect your device and choose storage and backup services that match your privacy needs.

7. Support and your privacy choices

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you send. We use this information to respond to your request and keep necessary support records. Email is handled by the email service providers involved in sending and receiving it. Please avoid sending sensitive media unless it is necessary for your request.

Support correspondence is retained only for as long as needed to address the inquiry, maintain necessary support records, and meet applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information you have sent us. We may need enough information to verify your request and may retain information where required by law. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

The app does not create user accounts, so there is no account to delete. You can control local app data using the steps in section 5.
